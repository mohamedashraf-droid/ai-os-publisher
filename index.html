AI OS Publisher

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

AI OS Publisher is a private tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and uploads the owner's own videos and images to the owner's own accounts.

  • YouTube: uploads and schedules videos (YouTube Data API).
  • TikTok: sends videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner publishes each one by hand (Login Kit and Content Posting API).
  • Facebook and Instagram: publishes posts the owner approved to the owner's own Page and Instagram account.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up or use it, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.
