Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

AI OS Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What the app accesses

  • When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to manage videos on that YouTube channel (YouTube Data API).
  • It uses this permission only to upload the owner's videos and set their title, description and publish time.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • It does not access any other person's account or data. No one else can sign up.
  • It does not send data to any third party other than Google/YouTube, which is needed to upload the videos.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted by the automation software (n8n). The owner can remove the app's access at any time from their Google Account (Security > Third-party apps with account access).

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. YouTube is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
